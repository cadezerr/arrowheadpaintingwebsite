Why it matters

Paint is your home's first line of defense

Kansas City is hard on exteriors. Summer sun bakes south and west walls, spring storms drive water into every gap, and winter freeze-thaw cycles open up joints and split old caulk. When paint starts peeling, chalking, or cracking, the wood underneath is exposed, and that's when rot starts.

A quality exterior repaint is about more than color. Done properly, it seals every seam, protects every board, and keeps small problems from turning into expensive siding and trim replacement.

Signs it's time to repaint

  • Peeling or flaking paint
  • Chalky residue when you touch the siding
  • Faded color on sunny sides
  • Cracked or missing caulk
  • Bare or graying wood
  • Soft spots in trim or siding

Power wash

Every surface is washed to remove dirt, mildew, and chalking so primer and paint actually bond.
