The process

Six steps, no surprises

  1. You reach out

    Call, text, or fill out our form. We'll reply within one business day to schedule your estimate.

  2. On-site estimate

    We walk the property with you, inspect for rot and failing caulk, and talk through goals and colors.

  3. Written quote

    An itemized proposal with your package and warranty, often in your inbox before we leave.

  4. Color consultation

    Professional color guidance and samples on your home so you can choose with confidence.

  5. Prep, repair & paint

    Power wash, wood repair, scrape, caulk, prime, and two finish coats. Daily updates from your project manager.

  6. Walkthrough & warranty

    We review every side with you, touch up anything you spot, and hand over your written warranty.
