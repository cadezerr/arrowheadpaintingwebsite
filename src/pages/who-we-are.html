Who we are

A locally owned painting company started by a Kansas painter who wanted customers to feel taken care of, not left guessing.

Meet Cade Zerr

Cade grew up in Kansas and graduated from the University of Kansas. He started painting in high school and kept working as a painter all through college, learning the trade one wall at a time.

After college he moved into managing commercial painting projects of every size, from $1,000 jobs to projects over $1 million. That experience showed him what the industry does well and where it falls short, especially in how contractors treat their customers.

He started Arrowhead Painting to do it differently: clear communication, honest recommendations, and a finished job you're proud to show off.

How we work

Open communication

You'll always know when we're arriving, what's happening next, and how the job is going. Questions get answered quickly.

Professional guidance

Not sure about colors, sheens, or which paint holds up best? We'll walk you through the options with a free color consultation.

Customized service

Every space is different. We plan the prep, products, and schedule around your project, whether it's one room or a whole building.
