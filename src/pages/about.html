Meet The Owner

Cade Zerr

Cade is a Kansas native and a graduate of the University of Kansas. He worked as a painter throughout high school and college, honing his skills on the job, and went on to manage commercial painting projects of every size.

Over the years, Cade saw where the painting industry fell short, especially in customer service. He founded Arrowhead Painting with a vision to change the way the industry operates: clear communication, honest recommendations, and a finished product customers are proud of.

Today, Arrowhead's team brings more than 35 years of combined experience to every project, and client satisfaction remains the top priority.
