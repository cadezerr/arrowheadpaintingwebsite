Meet the owner

Hi, I'm Cade.

I've been painting for 8 years. I grew up in Kansas, started painting in high school, and kept at it all the way through college at KU. After graduating, I managed commercial painting projects ranging from $1,000 touch-ups to jobs over $1 million.

Along the way I saw what separates a great paint job from one that fails in three years, and I saw how often homeowners were left guessing about schedules, costs, and what was actually happening at their house.

I started Arrowhead Painting to fix both. We do the prep and repairs properly, use the best coatings, and communicate like professionals from your first call to the final walkthrough. Two years and more than 200 projects later, that's still how we run every job.

Cade Zerr
Owner, Arrowhead Painting KC
