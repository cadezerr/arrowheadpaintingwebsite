How long does exterior paint last in Kansas City?

By Cade Zerr · September 29, 2026 · 6 min read

It's one of the first questions homeowners ask us: "If I paint now, how long until I have to do this again?" The honest answer is that it depends, but in Kansas City most well-done exterior paint jobs last 7 to 12 years. A few factors decide whether you land at the short end or the long end of that range.

Typical lifespan by siding type

  • Fiber cement (James Hardie): 10 to 15 years. It holds paint extremely well.
  • LP SmartSide and engineered wood: 8 to 12 years, as long as edges and seams stay sealed.
  • Cedar and other natural wood: 6 to 10 years. Wood moves the most, so caulk and prep matter more.
  • Stucco: 8 to 12 years with an elastomeric or masonry coating.
  • Trim, doors, and decks: trim often needs touch-ups sooner, and horizontal surfaces wear fastest of all.

What shortens paint life in Kansas City

Sun. UV breaks down paint over time, and the sunniest walls usually fade and chalk first. Freeze-thaw cycles. Our winters swing from mild to below zero, and every cycle opens up caulk joints and seams. Moisture. Spring storms push water into any gap, and once wood gets wet from behind, paint starts to peel. Poor prep. Paint applied over chalky, dirty, or loose paint simply won't bond, no matter how good the product is.

What makes paint last longer

  • Thorough prep: power washing, scraping, sanding, and priming bare wood.
  • Sealing every gap: quality caulk at every joint keeps water out of your siding.
  • Fixing rot first: painting over soft wood just hides the problem. See our wood rot repair.
  • Premium paint: higher-end lines hold color and flexibility longer, which is why our exterior warranties run 3, 5, or 7 years depending on the product.
  • Simple upkeep: rinse off dirt and mildew every year or two and touch up small spots before they spread.
