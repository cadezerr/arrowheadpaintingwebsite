What affects the cost of exterior painting in Kansas City?

By Cade Zerr · September 1, 2026 · 7 min read

"How much does it cost to paint a house?" is the first question almost everyone asks, and it's a fair one. The honest answer is that it depends on your house, and two quotes for the "same" job can be very different because they don't include the same work.

Here's what actually drives the price of an exterior paint job, and how to compare quotes so you're getting what you pay for.

1. Size and height of the home

More square footage means more surface to prep and paint. Height matters too: two-story walls, walkout basements, and steep lots need taller ladders and more time to work safely.

2. How much prep the house needs

Prep is the biggest difference between a paint job that lasts and one that peels in a few years. A house with tight, sound paint needs less work than one with heavy peeling, chalking, or failed caulk. Thorough prep includes:

  • Power washing every surface
  • Scraping and sanding loose paint
  • Caulking joints and gaps
  • Spot-priming bare wood and repairs
  • Masking windows, fixtures, and landscaping

3. Wood rot and siding repair

Most Kansas City homes have at least some rot on trim, door frames, or siding. Repairs are priced based on how much material needs replacing and what it's replaced with: cedar, LP SmartSide, James Hardie, AZEK, or PVC. A good contractor shows you the problem areas during the estimate and prices them in writing.

5. Details and color changes

Lots of trim, shutters, multiple accent colors, and detailed architecture take more time. A big color change, like going from dark to light, can also need extra coverage.

6. Access and site conditions

Steep slopes, decks, landscaping right against the house, and tight spaces between homes all affect how a job is set up.

How to compare painting quotes

The lowest number isn't a bargain if it leaves out the work that makes paint last. When comparing quotes, ask each contractor:

  1. Is power washing included?
  2. How will you handle wood rot? Replace it, or fill and paint over it?
  3. What paint product, specifically? Not just a brand, but the product line.
  4. What caulk do you use, and where?
  5. Is there a written warranty, and for how long?
  6. Are you insured?
  7. Who will I communicate with during the job?
