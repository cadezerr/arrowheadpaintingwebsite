How long does it take to paint a house exterior?

By Cade Zerr · October 2, 2026 · 5 min read

Once you've picked colors and signed off on a quote, the next question is usually "How long will the crew be here?" For most Kansas City homes, an exterior repaint takes a few days to about a week. Here's what drives that range.

A typical exterior painting schedule

  • Day 1: protect plants, walkways, and fixtures, then power wash the whole house.
  • Day 1 to 2: let surfaces dry, then scrape, sand, and replace any rotted wood.
  • Day 2 to 3: prime bare wood and caulk every gap and seam.
  • Day 3 to 5: paint siding, trim, soffits, fascia, doors, and garage doors.
  • Final day: touch-ups, cleanup, and a walkthrough with you.

What makes a project take longer

Size and height. A two-and-a-half or three-story home with lots of trim takes longer than a ranch. Wood rot. Replacing siding, fascia, or trim adds time, but it's what makes the paint last. Weather. Kansas City gets plenty of rain in spring and fall, and paint needs dry surfaces and the right temperatures to cure. We schedule around the forecast rather than rush it. Color changes. Going from dark to light, or making a dramatic change, can take an extra coat.
