Sherwin-Williams vs. Benjamin Moore: which exterior paint should you choose?

By Cade Zerr · August 25, 2026 · 6 min read

Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore are the two names homeowners ask about most, and for good reason. Both make excellent professional-grade exterior paints. We use both, but Sherwin-Williams is our go-to. Here's why, and how to think about the choice.

The short version

Both brands make top-quality exterior paint. The product line you choose within a brand matters more than the brand itself. A premium line from either company will outperform an entry-level line from the other. And no paint, however good, makes up for poor prep.

Why we lead with Sherwin-Williams

  • Consistent, proven products. We know exactly how SuperPaint, Duration, and Emerald Rain Refresh apply, cover, and hold up on Kansas City homes because we use them every week.
  • Local availability. Sherwin-Williams has stores across the metro, so we can get product, color matches, and touch-up paint quickly.
  • A clear good-better-best lineup that maps neatly to our 3-, 5-, and 7-year warranty packages.
  • Matching caulks. PowerHouse and SherMax elastomeric caulks pair with the coatings we use.

Our Sherwin-Williams exterior lineup

SuperPaint®

A reliable, proven exterior paint with good adhesion and color retention. It's a solid choice for budget-minded repaints and homes in good condition. Paired with PowerHouse caulk and our 3-year warranty.

Duration®

A thicker, more flexible film that hides better and stands up to weather. It's our most popular option and a great fit for most Kansas City homes. Paired with SherMax elastomeric caulk and a 5-year warranty.

Emerald® Rain Refresh

Sherwin-Williams' top-tier exterior coating. It's designed to shed dirt when it rains so the house stays cleaner, and it offers excellent color retention. It's ideal for darker colors, full-sun exposures, and homeowners planning to stay long-term. Paired with SherMax caulk and our 7-year warranty.

When Benjamin Moore makes sense

Benjamin Moore makes excellent exterior paints, and some homeowners love a specific Benjamin Moore color or have used it on previous projects. If you prefer it, we're happy to spec a comparable Benjamin Moore product. Your quote will show exactly what we'll use.

Color matching across brands

Found a color you love in the other brand? Paint stores can color-match across brands, though results vary slightly with sheen and base. We always recommend a sample on your actual siding before committing.

What matters more than the brand

  1. Prep: washing, scraping, caulking, and priming
  2. Repairs: replacing rotted wood instead of painting over it
  3. Application: the right film thickness, applied by experienced painters
  4. Conditions: painting in the right weather
