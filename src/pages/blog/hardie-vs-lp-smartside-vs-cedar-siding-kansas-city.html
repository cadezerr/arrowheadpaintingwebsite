James Hardie vs. LP SmartSide vs. cedar: choosing siding for repairs in Kansas City

By Cade Zerr · September 26, 2026 · 6 min read

When we find rotted siding during an estimate, the next question is what to replace it with. In Kansas City, most homes are built with one of a few materials, and the right choice depends on what's already on your house and how wet the area stays.

James Hardie fiber cement

Fiber cement is extremely durable, won't rot, and holds paint for a long time. It's a great choice for areas that see a lot of moisture and for homeowners who plan to stay put. It's heavier and takes more labor to install.

LP SmartSide engineered wood

LP SmartSide is common on newer Kansas City homes. It's lighter than fiber cement, resists impact well, and is the easiest way to match existing engineered siding. The key is sealing every cut edge and seam, because that's where water gets in.

Cedar

Cedar is natural wood with a classic look and natural rot resistance. It's the right match for cedar-sided homes and custom profiles, but it moves more with the seasons, so caulk and paint maintenance matter.

AZEK and PVC trim

For trim, door frames, and areas near the ground that never fully dry out, AZEK and PVC trim are hard to beat. They won't rot and take paint well when properly prepped.

Our approach: we recommend materials based on your wants and needs, then caulk, prime, and paint everything so the repair blends in with the rest of your home.
