How often should you paint the inside of your house?

By Cade Zerr · September 24, 2026 · Updated October 5, 2026 · 5 min read

Quick Answer

Every 5 to 10 years for most rooms. Hallways, kitchens, bathrooms, and kids' rooms usually need it every 2 to 5 years, and trim in busy areas every 3 to 5.

Interior paint doesn't face sun and storms, but it does face kids, pets, moving furniture, and everyday life. Most homes need interior painting every 5 to 10 years, but some rooms need it much sooner.

Room-by-room guide

  • Hallways, entryways, and mudrooms: every 2 to 4 years. These take the most bumps and scuffs.
  • Kids' rooms: every 3 to 5 years, or whenever their tastes change.
  • Kitchens and bathrooms: every 3 to 5 years, since moisture and cooking wear paint faster.
  • Living rooms, dining rooms, and bedrooms: every 5 to 10 years.
  • Ceilings: every 10 years or so, unless there are water stains.
  • Trim and doors: every 3 to 5 years in busy areas.
  • Cabinets: a quality cabinet paint job can last many years with normal care.

Signs it's time to repaint

Scuffs that won't wash off, fading, peeling in bathrooms, cracked caulk on trim, nail pops, or a color that just feels dated. If you're planning to sell, fresh neutral paint is one of the best-value updates you can make.
