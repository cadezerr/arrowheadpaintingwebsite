The most common thing we find

Most Kansas City homes have some rot. Most homeowners don't know it.

Rot starts small and hides well: the bottom of a door frame, the end of a trim board, a section of siding behind a downspout. By the time it's obvious, it has often spread to the boards around it.

That's why every exterior estimate includes a walk-around where we point out soft spots, failed caulk, and exposed wood. Homeowners tell us it's one of the most valuable parts of the visit.
